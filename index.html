Privacy Policy – YazoScan

Last updated: October 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how YazoScan handles information when you use the application. YazoScan is a barcode food scanner that shows product information and a simple score based on the product label.

1. Information We Collect

YazoScan does not require an account or sign-in. We do not collect your name, email address, password, contacts, or location, and we do not operate a server that stores your personal data.

2. Camera

YazoScan uses your device camera only to read product barcodes. Barcode detection is done on your device using Google ML Kit. Camera images are not saved, uploaded, or shared by the app.

3. Product Lookups (Open Food Facts)

When you scan or type a barcode, the barcode number is sent to the public Open Food Facts API (openfoodfacts.org) to fetch product information such as ingredients and nutrition values. The request is processed by Open Food Facts according to their own privacy policy. No photo of you or your surroundings is sent.

4. History and Favorites

Your scan history, favorites, and app settings are stored only on your device. They are not sent to us. You can clear your history at any time from the app, or remove all data by uninstalling the app.

5. Advertising

YazoScan displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and process certain device and advertising information, such as your advertising ID, IP address, and diagnostic data, according to Google's data practices. See Google's policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

6. No Sale of Data

We do not sell your personal data to anyone.

7. Not Medical Advice

Scores and product information in YazoScan come from public label data and are provided for general information only. They are not a medical diagnosis or professional dietary advice. Product data may be incomplete or inaccurate.

8. Children's Privacy

YazoScan is not directed to children under the age required by applicable law. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app, services, or applicable requirements change. The updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.

10. Contact
